The Parent actions page exposes existing Blackbaud tuition, PaySchools lunch, Brightwheel aftercare, teacher directory, Alma and athletics paperwork flows. Forms/signatures, volunteering and school support use clearly labeled office inquiries, not invented signup or payment workflows. No messages are sent automatically. No completion, balance or receipt is inferred from opening a link.

Important: official Adopt-A-Classroom link currently renders Vanco Unavailable Service. The general Online Giving link goes to the church’s Servant Keeper page, with school allocation not verified. Neither is represented as a working school donation checkout.

Announcements can reference a known action ID. The registry resolves approved destinations; arbitrary notice URLs do not become action links. Actual school requests are not connected. This is not native submission, digital signature or payment processing; provider authorization, supported APIs, verified delivery/receipt state and family scope are required before those capabilities can be presented as integrated.
